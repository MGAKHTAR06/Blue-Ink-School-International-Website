Our Curriculum

A broad and balanced curriculum designed to support academic excellence and personal development

Our curriculum is designed to provide students with strong academic foundations, excellent communication skills, and opportunities for wider personal development. Through a combination of core and optional subjects, we aim to prepare learners for future study, leadership, and meaningful contribution to society.

Core Subjects

English

Develops reading, writing, and communication skills essential for academic success.

Mathematics

Builds logical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and numerical confidence.

Biology

Explores living organisms, human health, and the natural world.

Chemistry

Introduces matter, reactions, and the science behind everyday substances.

Physics

Develops understanding of energy, forces, and how the physical world works.

Computer Science

Builds digital skills, coding knowledge, and understanding of modern technology.

Hindi

Enhances language skills and cultural understanding through reading and writing.

English Speaking

Improves confidence in communication, presentation, and spoken fluency.

Non-Verbal Reasoning

Develops logical thinking and problem-solving without relying on language.

Urdu

Promotes literacy and appreciation of language, culture, and literature.

Sports

Encourages physical fitness, teamwork, and a healthy lifestyle.

Arabic

Enhances language skills and cultural understanding through reading, writing, and communication.

Social Science

Explores people, communities, and society to build understanding of how the world works.

History

Examines past events, people, and civilizations to understand change over time.

Geography

Studies places, environments, and human interaction with the world around us.

Optional Classes

General Studies

Develops broad knowledge across a range of subjects, encouraging critical thinking and awareness of global topics.

Research About Issues in the Region and the Country

Students explore local and national issues, building research, analysis, and problem-solving skills.

Activities in Work Experience

Provides practical exposure to real-world environments, helping students develop workplace skills and confidence.

General Foundation Course

Strengthens core academic skills, preparing students for higher-level studies and future learning.

Visits to Monumental Places

Educational trips that enhance cultural understanding and bring history and heritage to life.

Looking After the Environment

Encourages environmental awareness and responsibility through activities focused on sustainability and conservation.
