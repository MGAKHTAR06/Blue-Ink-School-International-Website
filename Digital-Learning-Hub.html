Digital Learning Hub Launched

We are excited to introduce the Blue Ink School International Digital Learning Hub — free, interactive practice for every student, available any time.

Blue Ink School International is proud to launch its new Digital Learning Hub — a free online platform giving students access to interactive practice questions across key subjects. Each question is drawn directly from our curriculum books and comes with instant feedback, so students know immediately whether their answer is correct, along with a clear explanation to support their understanding.

How It Works

❓

Interactive Questions

Every question is taken directly from our curriculum books and displayed clearly with answer options for students to choose from.

⚡

Instant Feedback

As soon as a student submits their answer, they receive immediate feedback — correct or incorrect — along with a full explanation.

📊

Score Tracker

A live score tracker displays how many answers are correct, wrong, and the running percentage score throughout each practice session.
