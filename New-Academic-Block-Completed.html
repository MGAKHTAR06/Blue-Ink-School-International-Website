New Academic Block Completed

Our new academic building is now complete and ready for students, featuring modern science labs, a computer lab, a library, and dedicated classrooms.

Blue Ink School International is proud to announce the completion of its new academic building, designed to enhance the learning experience for all students. The facility includes three fully equipped science laboratories, a modern computer lab, a well-resourced library, and dedicated classrooms. This expansion reflects our commitment to providing high-quality infrastructure that supports both academic excellence and student development. The building is now fully operational and ready to welcome students into a modern and inspiring learning environment.

Our Facilities

Sewing and Practical Skills Training

Students are introduced to sewing and practical skills through guided lessons using sewing machines. This helps them develop creativity, patience, coordination, and valuable hands-on skills that can be useful in everyday life.

Dedicated Science Laboratories

Our school provides dedicated science laboratories equipped for practical experiments and hands-on learning. These labs enable students to explore scientific concepts in a structured environment, helping to build curiosity, understanding, and analytical skills.

Library

Our school library provides a quiet, well-organised, and supportive environment for reading, research, and independent learning. Students have access to a wide range of books and learning resources that help strengthen their knowledge, improve comprehension skills, and encourage a lifelong love for reading and education.
